I can’t sign in to my account|Make sure you’re using the same email address you used when creating your Idealize account. If you’re still unable to access your account, contact us at support@idealizepalace.com.
I didn’t receive my verification email|Check your spam or junk folder and make sure you entered the correct email address. Delivery can occasionally take a few minutes. If you still haven’t received the email, try requesting another verification email or contact support.
How do I manage or cancel my Idealize Pro subscription?|Subscriptions purchased through the iOS app are managed through Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, open: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Idealize. From there, you can view, manage, or cancel your subscription.
Does deleting Idealize cancel my subscription?|No. Deleting the app or your Idealize account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription. To prevent future renewals, manage your subscription through your Apple account.
How do I delete my Idealize account?|You can permanently delete your account directly from the Idealize app: You → Settings → Account → Delete Account. You’ll be asked to confirm before the account is deleted. Account deletion cannot be undone.
What happens to my ideas when I delete my account?|When account deletion is completed, your Idealize account and associated Idealize data are deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Locally stored Idealize data on your device is also removed as part of the account-deletion process.
Who owns the ideas I put into Idealize?|You do. You retain ownership of the ideas and content you create or upload to Idealize. Using Idealize does not transfer ownership of your ideas to us. For more information, see our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
