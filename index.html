Can't start?

Start with me.

Tap "Start with me" and Bubbles stays with you through a short timer while you do the first step.

  • Stuck? Ask for an even smaller step.
  • A thought pops up? Park it and keep going.
  • Too much today? Make today tiny: keep one thing, everything else moves to tomorrow. Nothing is late.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. If your head is busy and things keep slipping through, Idealize is for you.

Is Idealize a medical app?

No. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. It just holds things for you, so your head doesn't have to.

What happens after the free week?

You keep Idealize Free: typing, Today, reminders and your Palace, plus 3 spoken brain dumps a week. If you want more, you can go Pro. You never get charged unless you choose a plan.

Can I just type instead of talking?

Yes. Type it, snap a letter or talk. Idealize sorts it the same way.

How do I cancel Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Idealize. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription. More answers on the Support page.
