Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20/10/2026

Idealize respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, shared, and protected when you use the Idealize mobile application, website, and related services.

By using Idealize, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where consent is required for specific processing activities, we will ask for it separately.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Idealize, we may process information necessary to provide and operate the service.

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account identifier, and login or authentication details necessary to create, secure, and manage your account.

Content You Create

Idealize allows you to capture, organize, connect, and develop ideas.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

Ideas, titles, descriptions, and other text you enter.

Images and photographs.

Voice recordings and transcripts.

Documents, links, and attachments.

Rooms, themes, interests, and connections between ideas.

Information you voluntarily provide when using Idealize features.

Your content is stored within the application on your device. If you have Idealize Pro and Sync is turned on, your content is also stored in your private cloud space so it is available on every device where you sign in, as described in Section 5 (Cloud Sync). Certain account information is processed using our backend services, and content may be transmitted to external AI services when you explicitly enable AI processing and use the relevant features, as described below.

Subscription Information

If you purchase an Idealize subscription through the iOS application, payment is processed by Apple through the App Store.

Idealize does not receive or store your complete payment-card information.

We use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status and purchase entitlements. This may involve processing purchase identifiers, subscription status, and related transaction information. Your subscription status is also recorded in our backend so that Pro features, such as AI features and Cloud Sync, are available to your account.

Technical Information

We and our service providers may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Idealize.

This may include technical request information, diagnostic information, and IP addresses processed when communicating with online services.

When Cloud Sync is used, we also store technical information needed to keep your devices in step, such as a random device identifier, the device platform, and the last point each device synchronized to.

Idealize does not request access to your device’s GPS location or intentionally collect your precise or approximate geographic location through its app features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

Provide, maintain, and improve Idealize.

Create and authenticate user accounts.

Save, organize, and display your ideas.

Keep your ideas in step across your devices when you use Cloud Sync.

Provide features you request.

Process AI-assisted features when authorized.

Manage subscriptions and access to paid features.

Maintain security and prevent misuse.

Diagnose technical problems.

Respond to support requests.

Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your ideas or synced content for advertising, and we do not use them to train AI models.

3. Your Ideas Belong to You

You retain ownership of the ideas, projects, and other content you create or upload to Idealize.

Storing, syncing, or developing an idea using Idealize does not transfer ownership of that idea or content to us.

Your ideas are yours.

Idealize processes your content only as necessary to provide its functionality, including Cloud Sync and optional AI-assisted features you choose to enable.

4. AI Features and Data Processing

Certain Idealize Pro features use external artificial intelligence services to help develop, organize, transcribe, research, and connect your ideas.

AI processing is optional and requires your explicit permission.

Before enabling cloud AI processing, Idealize presents a consent screen explaining the types of information that may be shared and the external services involved.

Without this permission, Idealize prevents its cloud AI requests from being transmitted.

AI processing and Cloud Sync are separate. Turning on Cloud Sync does not send your ideas to AI services, and turning on AI processing does not turn on Cloud Sync.

Information Shared With AI Services

The information transmitted depends on the feature you choose to use.

Expand

When expanding an idea, Idealize may transmit its title, description, relevant attachments, selected image content, link information, document excerpts, voice transcripts, notes from related ideas, and a summary of your interests.

Research and Near Me

When requesting research or suggestions, Idealize may transmit the relevant idea’s title and description, your selected research direction, and previous suggestions where applicable.

The Near Me feature does not access your device’s GPS location.

Speak a Thought

When cloud AI processing is enabled, Idealize may transmit your voice recording for transcription and subsequently process the transcript alongside selected notes from your other ideas to help organize your thoughts.

If cloud AI processing is declined, compatible devices and languages may support local speech recognition without uploading the recording for cloud AI processing. If local recognition is unavailable, this functionality may be limited.

Voice Memos

Voice recordings may be transmitted to external transcription services when you have authorized cloud AI processing and use transcription functionality.

Without that permission, voice memos may be saved without cloud transcription.

Themes

When organizing ideas into themes, Idealize may transmit idea titles, short descriptions, branch names, and selected interests.

Without cloud AI permission, the application may offer locally generated theme suggestions instead.

Observatory

When enabled, the Observatory may transmit selected summaries of your ideas, recurring subjects, and interests to identify patterns and connections.

After you authorize AI processing, the Observatory may periodically refresh its analysis when you open the feature.

It may also process information about connected ideas when you focus on a particular thought.

These operations remain subject to your AI processing permission and applicable subscription access.

AI Infrastructure and Providers

To provide AI-assisted features, Idealize uses external infrastructure and AI services that may include:

Supabase — our backend, which checks your account and subscription and forwards your AI request.

Vercel — AI gateway infrastructure that routes the request to the AI provider.

Anthropic — AI language models.

Google — AI models and processing.

OpenAI — AI models and transcription.

xAI — AI processing and transcription.

Perplexity — research and information retrieval.

Depending on the feature, availability, and technical requirements, different providers may process the information necessary to fulfill your request.

Not every request is necessarily processed by every listed provider.

We do not intentionally include your account email address or name in AI prompts unless that information forms part of content you choose to submit.

AI Consent and Your Choices

You can manage cloud AI processing within the application by navigating to:

Settings → AI Processing

From this section, you can enable or disable AI processing.

When you disable AI processing:

New cloud AI requests are blocked.

In-progress requests are cancelled where technically possible.

Existing ideas, recordings, transcripts, and previously generated results remain available.

Features that require cloud AI may become unavailable or use local alternatives.

Withdrawing permission does not reverse processing that has already occurred or recall information already transmitted to an external provider.

If you later wish to re-enable AI processing, Idealize will request your permission again.

AI processing permission is associated with your account and is cleared when you sign out or delete your account.

AI Data Retention

External AI providers may temporarily process or retain information in accordance with their applicable policies, technical requirements, contractual arrangements, and legal obligations.

Retention periods and practices may differ between providers and services.

We do not guarantee that all AI providers immediately delete transmitted content after processing.

We do not represent that every provider operates under identical data-retention or model-training arrangements.

We aim to limit information shared with AI services to what is reasonably necessary for the requested functionality.

Apple Speech Recognition

Certain speech-recognition functionality may use Apple’s Speech framework.

Depending on the recognition mode, device capabilities, language support, and system configuration, Apple’s speech-recognition services may process audio on the device or through Apple’s servers.

Where Idealize specifically offers on-device-only recognition following a decision not to authorize cloud AI processing, that mode is intended to operate locally and may be unavailable if the device does not support it.

Apple’s processing is governed by its applicable privacy terms and policies.

5. Cloud Sync

Cloud Sync is coming in an upcoming update of Idealize. This section describes how it works once it is available in your app.

Cloud Sync keeps your Palace the same on every device where you sign in to your Idealize account. It is an Idealize Pro feature. Free accounts do not use Cloud Sync, and their content stays on the device.

What Is Synced

When Cloud Sync is on, the following are stored in your private cloud space: your ideas and their branches, titles, descriptions, and details; Rooms and themes; connections between ideas and the Observatory suggestions you kept or dismissed; your interests; your Palace progress; previously generated results saved with your ideas, such as Expand results; and the photos, voice recordings, and files attached to your ideas, up to 25 MB per file.

Turning Cloud Sync On or Off

Cloud Sync is on by default for Idealize Pro accounts. You can turn it off at any time within the application by navigating to:

Settings → Sync

When you turn Cloud Sync off, your devices stop exchanging changes. Your content stays on your device, and what was already synced stays in your private cloud space until you delete your account. To have your synced content removed without deleting your account, contact us using the information below.

If your Idealize Pro subscription ends, synchronization stops. Content already synced remains in your private cloud space until you delete your account.

When a Device and the Cloud Both Have a Palace

If a device you sign in to already holds a Palace and your account’s cloud space also holds one, Idealize asks you what to keep before anything is combined or replaced. Before carrying out your choice, Idealize makes a backup of that device’s Palace on the device.

Who Can Access Your Synced Content

Your synced content is private to your account. It is not public and is not shared with other users. Our backend only allows your signed-in account to read or add to your own cloud space.

Synced content is not sent to AI services unless you separately enable AI processing and use an AI feature, as described in Section 4.

Deleted Items

When you delete an idea or other item while Cloud Sync is on, it is removed from your devices. So that the deletion can reach all of your devices, a record of the deleted item may be kept in your cloud space, and files attached to deleted ideas may remain stored. These are removed when you delete your account.

7. Data Storage and Security

Your ideas are stored within the application on your device. If you use Cloud Sync, they are also stored in your private cloud space with our backend provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Ireland).

Account authentication and certain service operations rely on external infrastructure.

Information sent between the application and our services is encrypted in transit. Content stored with our backend provider is encrypted at rest. Access to synced content is limited to your signed-in account.

When you authorize cloud AI functionality, the information necessary for the selected feature is transmitted securely to the relevant service infrastructure for processing.

Idealize also keeps backups of your Palace on your device, for example before you sign out or before a Palace is replaced. These backups stay on your device.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Idealize, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, maintain security, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the information involved and the purpose of processing.

Content stored on your device generally remains available until you delete it, delete your account, or remove the application and its local data.

Content synced with Cloud Sync, including records of deleted items and attached files, is kept in your private cloud space until you delete your account, or until you ask us to remove it.

Information processed by external service providers, including AI providers, may be subject to separate retention periods.

Withdrawing AI consent prevents future cloud AI processing but does not necessarily delete information previously processed or retained by external providers.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your Idealize account directly within the application by navigating to:

Settings → Account → Delete Account

When account deletion is completed, the account and associated Idealize data under our control are deleted, including everything synced to your private cloud space and all attached photos, voice recordings, and files. This is subject to limited information that may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or legal purposes.

If your synced files cannot all be deleted, your account is not deleted and the application asks you to try again, so that no files are left behind.

Data stored locally by Idealize on your device is also removed as part of the application’s account-deletion process.

Information previously transmitted to external AI services may remain subject to those providers’ applicable retention and deletion practices.

Deleting your Idealize account does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription.

Apple subscriptions can be managed separately through your Apple account subscription settings.
